# Visitor-facing copy for Winter the Dolphin

Audience: general public, optimized for ~8-year-old reading level.
Voice: scannable bullets, plain text only for CMS (no Markdown, no emoji).
Layout: Glance & Match (Zone A static · Zone B art+copy · Zone C ticker).

## Zone A — Hero identification (static)

Winter the Dolphin
Tursiops truncatus

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Quick ID | Bottlenose dolphin · curved mouth · fast swimmer |
| Length | About 7 feet |
| Rescued | 2005 in Florida |
| Role | Rescue ambassador |

## Zone B — Paging insight cards (6s each · 24s loop)

Art sits in the upper band; copy sits in the lower band (never over the graphic).

### Card 1 — Diet

- Eat fish, squid, and shrimp
- Hunt with clicks to find prey
- Adults may eat many pounds each day

### Card 2 — Threat

- Fishing line can wrap tails tightly
- Plastic and trash pollute their homes
- Fast boats can strike dolphins

### Card 3 — Hidden Fact

- Winter lost her tail in a crab trap line
- She swam with a custom prosthetic tail
- Her story inspired books and a film

### Card 4 — How to Help

- Never throw fishing line in the water
- Report hurt or stranded sea animals
- Share what you learned today

## Zone C — Continuous ticker

One short CTA, slowly scrolling (do not pack multiple messages into the band):

Support marine mammal rescue and rehabilitation near you

## Call to action (author notes)

Why rescue centers matter (for educators; ticker stays one short line):

- Rehab teams heal sick and injured dolphins
- Science from rescue helps wild populations
- Public visits fund food, medicine, and care
