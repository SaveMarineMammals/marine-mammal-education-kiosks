# Visitor-facing copy for Red Mangrove — The Walking Tree

Audience: general public, optimized for ~8-year-old reading level.
Voice: scannable bullets, plain text only for CMS (no Markdown, no emoji).
Layout: Glance & Match (Zone A static · Zone B art+copy · Zone C ticker).

## Zone B — Paging insight cards (6s each · 24s loop)

Art sits in the upper band; copy sits in the lower band (never over the graphic).

### Card 1 — Stilt Roots

- Roots look like legs in the water
- Natural stilts help the tree stand
- They hold the tree up where waves hit

### Card 2 — Fish Nursery

- Baby fish, crabs, and shrimp hide here
- Roots keep little animals safe from hunters
- Can you spot animals hiding in the roots?

### Card 3 — Hidden Fact

- Seeds sprout while still on the tree
- Baby trees float away like little darts
- Roots soften waves in big storms

### Card 4 — How to Help

- Keep trash and plastic out of water
- Support mangrove restoration nearby
- Share why the walking tree matters

## Zone C — Continuous ticker

One short CTA, slowly scrolling (do not pack multiple messages into the band):

Protect mangroves — coasts, nurseries, and manatees need them

## Call to action (author notes)

Why protect mangroves (for educators; ticker stays one short line):

- Protect coasts from storms
- Provide nursery habitats for marine life
- Store up to 4x more carbon than many forests
- Support species like the Greater Caribbean manatee
